## UltimatiumTube Stable v1.18.1

**Which file:** UltimatiumTube is supported on Tizen 3.0 and newer.
- Tizen 5 or under (3.0, 4.0, 5.0): `UltimatiumTubetizen5.0.wgt`
- Tizen 5.5 or above: `UltimatiumTubetizen5.5.wgt`
- Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV: `UltimatiumTube.apk`

Coming from v1.18: a TV on v1.18 gets this by itself (self-update) the next time the app opens after it has been open a little while; installing the files works too. Coming from anything older: reinstall on Samsung TVs, and on Android uninstall the old app first (the APK is signed with a new key since v1.18).

### New
- **Welcome to UltimatiumTube** has **Close** and **Don't show again** beside the three modes (UltimatiumTube, SmartTube UI, YT Dummy). Back closes it too. After Don't show again it no longer opens with the app; Settings > UltimatiumTube > Profiles and what's new shows it any time and has the switch to bring it back.

### Everything from v1.18
- **UltimatiumTube mode is the real YouTube app**: YouTube's TV app as Samsung's own YouTube app gets it, so Shorts play in the Shorts player one after another and search works as in the app. If the app's service does not answer within a few seconds, YouTube loads directly, so the app always opens.
- **SmartTube UI**: UltimatiumTube's own home screen in SmartTube's manner, with its own quick search.
- **YT Dummy**: YouTube exactly as youtube-dummy loads it.

The `.wgt` files are unsigned: install them with Apps2Samsung, Tizen Homebrew or Tizen Studio, which sign them for your TV.
