## UltimatiumTube Stable v1.17

**Which file:** UltimatiumTube is supported on Tizen 3.0 and newer.
- Tizen 5 or under (3.0, 4.0, 5.0): `UltimatiumTubetizen5.0.wgt`
- Tizen 5.5 or above: `UltimatiumTubetizen5.5.wgt`
- Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV: `UltimatiumTube.apk`

### New
- **SmartTube UI, for real.** A home screen of UltimatiumTube's own in the manner of SmartTube: a side menu with UltimatiumTube's name and mark, rows of your videos (Home, Subscriptions, Library, History, Music, Gaming, News, Sports, Live, Podcasts, Movies & TV), red focus, a clock. Videos play in YouTube's own player; Back comes home again. *Classic YouTube* in the menu shows YouTube's own home (to sign in, for one).
- **Welcome to UltimatiumTube** every time the app opens, with **Stock YouTube UI** or **SmartTube UI**. Choosing one now loads YouTube afresh in it.
- **Loads YouTube straight**, the way youtube-dummy does: no Cobalt container and no service to wait on, so no more host errors.
- **Like YouTube's own TV app:** full animations, Shorts in their own player, quicker search suggestions, a remote that answers at once.
- **Self-updating:** new versions of UltimatiumTube arrive from GitHub by themselves and start the next time the app opens.
- **Speed up to 20x**, labelled UltimatiumTube beyond YouTube's own 2x.
- **Stay signed in** when the TV's network address changes.

### Fixed
- SmartTube style no longer recolours YouTube's own screens (buttons were unreadable).
- The Background colour setting shows (YouTube's own page forced black over it).

The `.wgt` files are unsigned: install them with Apps2Samsung, Tizen Homebrew or Tizen Studio, which sign them for your TV.
